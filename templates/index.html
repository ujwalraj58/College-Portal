About Our College

Learn about our history, vision and mission.

🏫

History

Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Hyderabad (JNTUH) aims to advance technical education and research to benefit both the state and the country. Established in 1972, JNTUH operates from a sprawling 89-acre campus in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, serving over 250,000 students across Telangana.

🌟

Vision

To emerge as a world-class hub for technical education and research. By synergizing teaching with modern research, JNTUH strives to meet the technological and socioeconomic needs of the state and the nation.

🎯

Mission

  • Advance Technical Excellence
  • Quality Education
  • Research and Development
  • Values and Ethics
